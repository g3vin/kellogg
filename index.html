Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 · Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kellogg ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS messaging program and related services. Kellogg is the registered brand associated with this messaging program.

Who we are

Kellogg operates a small, personal telephone service. Our phone number receives text messages and voice calls from friends, family, and individuals who choose to contact us. This is a non-commercial service operated by an individual.

What information we collect

We collect only what is necessary to receive and respond to your messages:

  • Your phone number, as provided automatically by your mobile carrier when you send us a message or place a call.
  • Message content, meaning the text and any images you choose to send us.
  • Timestamps recording when a message or call was sent or received.
  • Opt-in and opt-out status, so we can honor your preferences.
  • Contact names, where you have told us how you would like to be identified, or where we have saved a name alongside your number.

We do not collect location data, browsing activity, advertising identifiers, payment information, or any data from your device beyond what you send us directly.

How we use your information

  • To receive, read, and reply to messages you send us.
  • To place and receive voice calls you have requested or initiated.
  • To identify you by name when you contact us, so we can respond appropriately.
  • To honor opt-out requests and maintain records of messaging preferences.

Messages you send may be displayed or printed on physical equipment located at our private residence as part of the normal operation of this service. Messages are not published, posted publicly, or shared beyond the household operating this number.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use Twilio Inc. as our telecommunications service provider to transmit and receive messages and calls. Twilio processes this information solely to deliver the service on our behalf, under its own privacy policy. We do not provide your information to advertisers, data brokers, marketing partners, or any other third party.

We may disclose information only where required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request from a law enforcement or regulatory authority.

How you opt in

You opt in to receive messages from us by sending a text message to our phone number. The act of texting us is your consent to receive a reply. We do not collect, purchase, import, or message phone numbers that have not contacted us first, and we do not add anyone to a messaging list.

How you opt out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive a single confirmation message, after which we will not send you further messages. You can opt back in at any time by texting us again. Reply HELP for assistance.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies and depends entirely on the conversation you initiate. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Data retention

We retain message content and contact information only as long as needed to operate the service. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below, and we will remove it promptly unless we are legally required to retain it.

Security

Information is stored on equipment under our direct physical control and is not uploaded to any public service or third-party database. While no system can be guaranteed completely secure, we take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we hold.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact us
