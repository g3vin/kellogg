Text the Phone

A 1900s Kellogg magneto wall telephone, rebuilt to answer, dial, and print.

Messages sent to this number are printed on a thermal receipt printer inside the cabinet of a restored hand-crank telephone. It is a personal project, not a business. Nothing is sold here and no marketing is ever sent.

Text KELLOGG to (757) 383-9589

You will get one message back asking you to confirm. Reply Y and you are signed up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

What happens when you text

Your message prints on paper in a wooden box on a wall. That is the whole feature. You may also call the number and speak to whoever picks up the handset.

What we do with your number

We keep it so we can reply to you, and that is all. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. The full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Stopping

Reply STOP to any message and you will not hear from us again. Reply HELP for help. There is no catch and nothing to unsubscribe from beyond the conversation itself.
